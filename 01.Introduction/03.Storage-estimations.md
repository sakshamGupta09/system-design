What are my storage requirements per day.

- **Thousands:** 10³

- **Billions:** 10⁹  
- **Trillions:** 10¹²

## Storage Estimation

**Formula:**

Storage per day = Items created per day × Average size per item
---

### Example: Social Media Posts

Assume a platform generates:
- 500 million text posts per day.
- Average post size: 300 bytes.
- 10% of posts include an image.
- Average image size: 4 MB.

**Text storage:**

500 million × 300 bytes ≈ **150 GB/day**

**Image storage:**

50 million × 4 MB ≈ **200 TB/day**


## Replication and Redundancy

Why? To prevent data loss and keep the system available if a server fails.

Systems often store multiple copies of data across different servers or locations.

Rule of thumb: If 3 total copies are stored, multiply the original storage by 3.
