# System Design: 4-Step Framework

- If we are asked to build a system (example Twitter), How do we start?
- Below is a template we can use to approach any system design problem.

## The 4 Steps

1. **Gather Requirements** — Understand what to build.
2. **High-Level Design** — Identify the major components and how they interact.
3. **Detailed Design** — Design the critical components in depth.
4. **Scaling & Optimization** — Identify bottlenecks and make the system reliable and scalable.


## Final Checklist

Before finishing, ask yourself:

- [ ] Have I clarified the functional and non-functional requirements?
- [ ] Have I drawn the major components and data flows?
- [ ] Have I explained the critical components in detail?
- [ ] Have I identified bottlenecks and discussed scaling?
- [ ] Have I considered failures, monitoring, and cost?
- [ ] Have I explained the trade-offs behind my decisions?

Through all 4 steps remember to engage with the interviewer.

- Does this approach makes sense?
- Should I dive deeper into a component?
- Are there specific concerns you would like me to address?
