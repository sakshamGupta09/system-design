# What Is System Design?

Imagine we are building a social media application that needs to support millions of users.

- How do we handle millions of concurrent users?
- How do we ensure fast response times, even under heavy traffic?
- How do we prevent failures and keep the system available?
- How do we scale the system as the number of users grows?

**System design** is the process of understanding business requirements and translating them into a technical architecture that defines how a system's components work together to meet those requirements.

The goal is to build systems that are **scalable, performant, reliable, and maintainable**.


## Trade offs

- There's no perfect system. Every decision has pros and cons.
- As an architect, we should understand the trade-offs and choode the right one for our situation.
