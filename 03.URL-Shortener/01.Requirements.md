# Functional Requirements

- Convert a long URL to a short one (minimum characters possible).
- Visiting short URL should take user to the original URL.
- Custom short URLs. Users can choose their own unique ID in the URL.
- Links should expire after 2 years.
- Analytics. Track the clicks count, geographic distribution and device types.

## NFRs (Quality attributes)

- **Performance/ Latency** Short URL must redirect to original URL in less than 200ms.
- **Availability** of 99.9%. If the service is down all the short URLs stop working.
- **Scalability** 100 Billion URLs.

## Constraints and Assumptions

- 100 million URLs per month.
- Read to write ration is 100:1 For every URL created, we click it 100 times.
