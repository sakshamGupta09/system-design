# Trade offs

The ACID properties comes with a performance cost.
- Ensuring the atomicity means you need to maintain those transaction logs.
- The consistency requires checking all your constraints.
- The isolation needs either the locking or the versioning mechanisms.
- And the durability requires disk writes.

Some systems choose to relax certain properties which are not absolutely required.

## Examples

### Redis - Durability

- Redis can be configured to write to the disk only every few seconds instead of immediately.
- You lose the durability for the most recent writes if there's a crash, but you gain the 10 times better performance in return.


### Social Media - Consistency

- The social media platforms often relax the consistency for the features like the like count on a post.
- Let's say a post shows 1,000 likes to one user and 1,001 likes to another user at the same moment.
- Honestly, nobody really cares about that tiny inconsistency. The performance gain you get from the eventual consistency is absolutely worth that minor discrepancy.
